Photo Effects Demo

Ten small effects for avatars and photos. Effects 1 to 5 play on their own for under 2 seconds, then rest for about 10 seconds. Effects 6 to 10 react when you hover over the photo or reach it with the Tab key. Every effect stops or stays still if your device is set to reduce motion.

See the code for every effect

On a dark background

  • 1. Breathing Glow Ring

    Plays on its own · Circle

    A soft ring around the photo brightens and grows a little, then rests.

  • 2. Rotating Gradient Border

    Plays on its own · Circle

    A colored gradient border makes one full turn, then holds still.

  • 3. Soft Pulse Halo

    Plays on its own · Circle

    One thin ring drifts out from the photo and fades away. It is drawn outside the photo without being cut off.

  • 4. Corner Glint

    Plays on its own · Circle

    A small four-point sparkle grows and turns on the edge of the photo, then disappears.

  • 5. Scan Line

    Plays on its own · Rounded rectangle

    A soft band of light slides down over the photo once, then rests.

  • 6. Tilt And Lift

    Hover over it or press Tab to reach it · Rounded rectangle

    The photo tips toward you and rises, and a shadow fades in under it.

  • 7. Zoom In Frame

    Hover over it or press Tab to reach it · Rounded rectangle

    The photo grows a little inside its frame, so the frame stays still.

  • 8. Grayscale To Color

    Hover over it or press Tab to reach it · Circle

    The photo starts in black and white and fades to full color.

  • 9. Corner Frame Draw-In

    Hover over it or press Tab to reach it · Rounded rectangle

    Two corner brackets grow in from the corners, outside the photo.

  • 10. Offset Frame Slide

    Hover over it or press Tab to reach it · Rounded rectangle

    An outline frame sits behind the photo, a little to the side. On hover the photo and frame slide apart.
