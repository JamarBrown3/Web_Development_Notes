What to Know Before You Buy a Graphics Card

By Jane Doe·October 21, 2026·6 min read

The graphics card does most of the work in games and in a lot of creative software, so it is worth getting right.

Match the card to your screen

A card that is perfect for a 1080p monitor can struggle at 4K. Decide on your screen resolution and refresh rate first, then look at benchmarks for the games you play at that resolution.

Check the size

High-end cards can be very long and take up three slots. Measure the space in your case before you buy.

Check the power

Look up the recommended power supply for the card, and make sure your supply has the right power connectors.

Tip Last generation's high-end card can be better value than this generation's mid-range card. Compare benchmarks, not model numbers.
