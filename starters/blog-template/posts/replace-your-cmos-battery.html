How to Replace Your CMOS Battery

By Jane Doe·September 30, 2026·3 min read

A small coin battery on the motherboard keeps the clock running and remembers firmware settings while the computer is unplugged. After several years it can run out.

Signs the battery is low

  • The date and time reset when the computer is unplugged.
  • Firmware settings go back to defaults.
  • The computer shows a battery warning when it starts.

Steps

  1. Shut down, switch off the power supply and unplug the computer.
  2. Touch a metal part of the case to discharge static.
  3. Find the round battery on the motherboard. Most boards use a CR2032.
  4. Press the small clip and lift the old battery out.
  5. Put the new one in with the same side facing up.
  6. Plug in, start up and set the date and your settings again.
Check first Your motherboard manual shows where the battery is and which type it takes.
