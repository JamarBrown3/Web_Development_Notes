Picking a PC Case That Fits Everything

By Jane Doe·October 14, 2026·4 min read

The case is the one part you see every day, but it also decides how cool and quiet your computer runs.

Size comes first

Cases are sized to match motherboards. A mid tower fits a standard ATX board and suits most builds. Smaller cases look great but leave less room for big graphics cards and coolers.

Airflow

A mesh front panel lets fans pull in cool air. A solid glass front often looks cleaner but can run warmer.

Quick checklist

  • Maximum graphics card length
  • Maximum CPU cooler height
  • Radiator sizes it supports, if you want liquid cooling
  • Space behind the motherboard for cables
